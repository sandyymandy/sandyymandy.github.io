PleasureCraft

About the Mod

PleasureCraft is an adult-themed mod for Minecraft 1.21.4 and above made using the Fabric. It’s a full remake and modern reimagining of the old 1.12.2 FapCraft mod made by Schnurri_tv

Features

Custom GUI

  • A 4x3 inventory slot system.
  • Buttons for commands and interactions.
  • Works only for the owner.

    • Scenes

  • Enter interactive sex scenes with your companion.
  • Progress bars appear, fill them to advance.
  • You can exit a scene anytime with the crouch key

    • Respawn System

  • Girls respawn instead of permanently dying.
  • Respawn location is dependent on where her base is set.
  • By default it is the place you tamed her but this can be changed by clicking the set base here button in her GUI.

    • Clothing & Armor

  • Girls can wear custom clothing and armor.
  • Clothing can be stripped off or put back on.
  • Armor provides protection and is also visible on the entity.
